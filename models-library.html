Antomology

Six models in order, from 2 blocks to 13. Your students start with a single ant looking for food and build up to a full colony with pheromones, a life cycle and predators.

Antomology - Introduction

In this game, two colonies of ants forage for food. Though each ant follows a set of simple rules, the colonies as a whole act in sophisticated ways. Ant Adaptation simulates two ant colonies side by side, each controlled by a different player.

Gases and particles

Your students program how gas particles move and collide, then use the pattern they see to explain gas behavior and heat transfer.

Chemistry and PhysicsLessonStep 1 of 2

Two Particles in a Box

This model enables students to draw a model to "sketch" up representations of new systems to explore concepts related to gas behavior and gas particles. A wide range of real world systems can be modeled with this simple interface (e.g. diffusion of perfume from an uncapped container, hot gas mixed with a cold gas, mixtures of gases).

By Umit Aslan

Spaceship programming

Two models in order. Your students schedule engine firings to fly a spaceship to a target, then set the force of each engine.

Students schedule timed engine firings with blocks to fly a spaceship to a target. The ship keeps its velocity until an opposing engine fires.
