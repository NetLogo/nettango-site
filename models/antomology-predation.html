Antomology - Predation

By Center for Connected Learning

In this game, two colonies of ants forage for food. Though each ant follows a set of simple rules, the colonies as a whole act in sophisticated ways. Ant Adaptation simulates two ant colonies side by side, each controlled by a different player.

Open in BuilderDownload project file

Tips for class

  • Two spiders start in the bottom right corner. A spider eats an ant on its spot with a chance of 100 minus 3 times the ant's size (percent), so bigger ants survive more often.
  • Bigger ants cost more: Create Cost is 2 plus start-energy divided by 1000 plus half the ant size. Set size on hatch-ants and compare the Ants and Spiders monitors.
  • Spiders make a new spider after eating, so ask your students whether spider numbers will follow ant numbers on the population plot.
